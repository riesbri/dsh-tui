Ease a newly arrived live bar in over a few redraws instead of flashing straight to full width — the first span is always the longest, so pure measurement drew every arrival at maximum. The ease advances on the live area's existing redraw cadence, adds no timer, and never alters the measured duration beside the bar; spans that predate the panel appearing draw at full width immediately.
