Ease a newly arrived live bar in over a few working heartbeats instead of flashing straight to full width — the first span is always the longest, so pure measurement drew every arrival at maximum. The ease follows the working spinner's existing heartbeat rather than render counts, so bursts of streamed redraws cannot spend it; it adds no timer and never alters the measured duration beside the bar, and spans that predate the panel appearing draw at full width immediately.
